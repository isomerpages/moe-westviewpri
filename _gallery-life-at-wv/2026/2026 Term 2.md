<h2>14. The 2026 National School Games (NSG) Senior Division</h2>
<p>The 2026 National School Games (NSG) Senior Division concluded last week,
and West View Primary is bursting with pride for our student-athletes.
This year’s competition was fierce, featuring more than 100 schools across
many leagues.</p>
<p><strong>Basketball (Senior Boys)</strong>
<br>Our squad of 14 boys stood tall in a highly competitive field. Earning
a spot in League 2, the boys displayed remarkable resilience and determination.
After a series of grueling matches, they advanced to the League 2 Finals,
finishing as Runners-Up (2nd Place).</p>
<p><strong>Football (Senior Boys)</strong>
<br>Our footballers gave their absolute all, narrowly missing the semi-finals
in their league by a single placing. More importantly, they were true ambassadors
of our TALENT values. Their journey was a victory of character and grit.</p>
<p><strong>Floorball (Senior Girls)</strong>
<br>The Senior Girls team delivered a masterclass in perseverance. Competing
in League 2, our 16 representatives faced tough opposition with unwavering
spirit. We are thrilled to announce that their hard work culminated in
a historic finish— 1st Place in League 2!</p>
<p>Congratulations to all our pupils for their outstanding effort, teamwork
and sportsmanship!</p>
