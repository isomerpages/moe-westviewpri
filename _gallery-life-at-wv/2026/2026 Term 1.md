<h2>8. Chinese New Year Celebration at WVPS</h2>
<p>Our school joyfully welcomed the Year of the Horse with a series of exciting
and festive activities!</p>
<p>The celebration began with engaging classroom activities, where pupils
explored Chinese New Year traditions through hands-on arts and crafts and
interactive learning experiences. Our MK children confidently presented
a mini skit showcasing how families welcome guests, enjoy festive delicacies,
and toss yu sheng together.
<br>This was followed by a vibrant hall celebration, featuring spectacular
performances such as an energetic lion dance, large flag twirling, dazzling
acrobatics, and captivating performances by both pupils and teachers.
<br>Through these festivities, pupils deepened their appreciation of Chinese
New Year customs while celebrating together as a joyful school community.</p>
<p>Wishing everyone a Galloping Horse Year filled with energy, progress,
and success!</p>
