<h2><strong>Learning for Life Programme</strong></h2>
<p>The Learning for Life Programme at West View Primary School seeks to instill
in pupils, a passion for learning for life and an appreciation for the
arts. Pupils learn how to express themselves and create opportunities for
themselves. They are given many opportunities to explore and develop their
strengths and interests, using their gifts to serve the school and community.</p>

<h3><strong>Aesthetics Week</strong></h3>
<p>Aesthetics Week was an explosion of creativity, offering pupils a wide
range of exciting artistic experiences. From community colouring and recess
karaoke to quick-draw self-portraits and portrait tracing, every activity
encouraged self-expression, imagination, and artistic exploration. The
immersive experience further captivated pupils, transporting them into
a world of creativity and inspiration.</p>

<p>The week culminated in the vibrant Fan Parade, where pupils proudly showcased
their uniquely designed fans. Using recycled and everyday materials, they
transformed ordinary items into meaningful works of art, demonstrating
both creativity and a commitment to sustainability. Aesthetics Week concluded
on a high note, celebrating the diverse talents of every West Viewan. More
than just a week of activities, it inspired pupils to express themselves
confidently, appreciate the arts, and embrace creativity as an integral
part of their learning journey.</p>

<h3><strong>STRIVE Programmes</strong></h3>
<p>Pupils are immersed in various dance and music experiences, including
K-pop dance, ethnic dance, hip hop, beatboxing, and songwriting workshops.
These enriching opportunities nurture creativity, confidence, and self-expression,
while providing holistic learning experiences that inspire pupils to develop
new skills, appreciate diverse cultures, and embrace learning for life.</p>
